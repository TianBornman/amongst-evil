# High Thalenor

*The Ruins Where the Brotherhood Was Born.*

> *"In the chaos, the now declared traitors of the brotherhood and some of the rulers of the shattered realms met beneath the ruins of High Thalenor. There they reforged the order — no longer an army, but a brotherhood unbound by crown, morals, or conscience."*

High Thalenor is the site of the Brotherhood's founding — the ruins where the defiant members of the [[The Sanctum Vigil|Sanctum Vigil]] and the rulers of shattered realms gathered at the end of the [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering of the Crown]] to forge something new from what the King's betrayal had broken.

---

## What It Was

High Thalenor is referred to only as **ruins** in Brotherhood records — its original form, purpose, and the nature of its fall are not established in surviving texts. It was a place already broken before the Brotherhood arrived, which is precisely why they chose it.

No king ruled High Thalenor. No throne claimed it. No army garrisoned it. It had no authority that could name the gathered Brothers traitor or heretic — because it had no authority at all.

---

## The Founding

Beneath High Thalenor's ruins, the Brotherhood's founding creed was **carved into the stone**:

> *"We stand where others flee. We kill what cannot die. We use what should never be touched."*

This was not a declaration to the world. There was no ceremony, no audience, no announcement. The creed was carved into stone underground because the people who carved it could not afford to be found. It was a statement made to themselves, by themselves, to last regardless of whether they did.

---

## Who Was Present

The founding gathering included:
- Surviving defiant members of the Sanctum Vigil — scholars, warriors, and magi who had refused to recant
- **Rulers of shattered realms** — kings and leaders whose kingdoms had been broken by the Curse and by [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|Alexandr's]] failures

The inclusion of shattered-realm rulers is significant: the Brotherhood was not founded by survivors alone, but by survivors and by those who still held some form of legitimate authority. Whatever that legitimacy was, they brought it to High Thalenor and placed it beneath a creed that deliberately discarded crowns.

---

## Present Status

High Thalenor's current state — whether it remains ruins, has been occupied, built upon, or further destroyed — is not established in Brotherhood records beyond the founding account.
