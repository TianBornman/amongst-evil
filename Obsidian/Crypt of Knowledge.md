# The Crypt of Knowledge

> [!quote] Fourth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "In blood, we learn. In death, we remember. In darkness, we preserve."

The Crypt of Knowledge is not a library. Libraries are for the public. Libraries can be burned.

The Crypt is carved beneath the Brotherhood's fortress — accessible only to those bound by oath — a vault built not to organise information, but to **ensure it cannot be erased**. It holds what the world is not allowed to remember: the true cause of the Curse, the forging of the [[Grand Clock]], the sins of the crown that began it all.

---

## Origin

The Crypt was built during the earliest years of the Brotherhood — in the aftermath of the [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering of the Crown]], when [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|the King]] rewrote the scriptures, erased records, and commanded the temples to name [[Akalaer|Akalaer's]] death divine judgment.

The Brotherhood's response was not argument. It was **preservation**.

If the King could erase truth from the world above, the Brotherhood would bury truth beneath it — in blood-bound tomes no royal decree could touch, in a vault guarded by the restless souls of Brothers who swore never to let its contents fade.

> *"They built The Crypt of Knowledge, a blood-bound vault to preserve what the world was not allowed to remember."*

---

## What It Contains

The Crypt holds:
- **Blood-bound tomes** written in the ink of the fallen — the collective knowledge of every Brother who ever served and died
- **Sealed reliquaries** containing fragments of truth too dangerous or too fragile to leave open
- **Scrolls of Mortality** — the Brotherhood's living record of every initiate who has ever taken the [[Blood Vault#The Rite of Names|Rite of Names]]; part of the [[Blood Vault]] system, housed here
- **The Curse chronicle** — a running record of the Curse's spread, its variants, its effects on specific locations and populations
- **Grand Clock records** — the Brotherhood's ongoing documentation of the Clock's tempo, acceleration, and any observable changes
- **Personal Tombs** of fallen Brothers — recovered, verified, and preserved here permanently
- **Relic catalogues** — documented properties of every relic recovered by Brotherhood Creeds from [[Brotherhood Progression#Rank 5 — Eclipsed Sigil|Rank 5]] onward
- **The sins of [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|the crown]]** — the original proof uncovered in the [[Tomb of the First Sin]]: that Alexandr killed Akalaer and claimed forbidden power

---

## Access

The Crypt is not open to all Brothers. Access is restricted by rank on the [[Brotherhood Progression#The Spiral of the Veil|Spiral of the Veil]]:

| Access Level | Rank Required | What They May Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading access** | Eclipsed Sigil (5) | General archive; Curse chronicle; relic catalogues |
| **Full access** | Shadowed Archive (7) | All tomes; sealed reliquaries; Tomb archive |
| **Restricted archive** | Obsidian Hand (8)+ | Contents of sealed reliquaries; the King's original sin documents |
| **The Unwritten Vault** | Clockless (10) | *Unknown — rumoured to exist below the Crypt itself* |

Magisters — Brotherhood scholars stationed at the Crypt — manage access, verify Tomb blood-signatures, and maintain the ongoing records.

---

## The Atmosphere

The Crypt's halls are lit by **ghostlight** — candles that burn with the cold blue fire said to be kindled from the souls of Brothers who swore never to let the knowledge fade. The same blue flame burns in the [[Blood Vault]] chamber beneath.

Pages are written with the ink of the fallen. Every tome is sealed with the blood of the Brother who wrote it. The Crypt does not merely hold records — it *holds the people who made them*, in the only way the Brotherhood knows how.

> [!quote]
> *"Only trusted Brotherhood members may walk its halls, where pages are written with the ink of the fallen and the candles burn with ghostlight."*

---

## The Tomb Verification Ritual

When a fallen Brother's **[[Blood Vault#Tombs of Blood and Ink|Tomb]]** is recovered and brought to the Crypt, magisters perform a verification ritual:

1. Blood from the Tomb's pages is tested against the corresponding entry in the Scrolls of Mortality
2. **If the blood matches** — if the Tomb belongs to a soul bound by oath — the knowledge within is preserved, copied, and added to the eternal record
3. **If the blood does not match** — if the writer was never bound by the Vault — the book is burned, and the ashes cast to the Grand Clock's wind

This is not cruelty. It is the Eighth Oath in practice:

> [!quote] Eighth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "Knowledge not born of oath is rot."

---

## In-Game — Camp Unlock

The Crypt of Knowledge becomes a physical camp presence at **[[Brotherhood Progression#Rank 7 — Shadowed Archive|Shadowed Archive (Rank 7)]]** — a Crypt annex is added to the camp alongside a permanent magister station.

However, the Crypt exists as a background system from Rank 5 onward — recovered relics are catalogued there automatically, and fallen Brothers' Tombs are transported there when camp processes mission aftermath.

> [!note] Design Intent
> The Crypt should feel like a place the player earns access to — not a tutorial screen. By the time a Creed reaches Rank 7 and sees the Crypt annex take shape in their camp, they should already have *lost* Brothers whose Tombs now live there. The Crypt is not a reward. It is a consequence.
