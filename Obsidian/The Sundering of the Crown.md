# The Sundering of the Crown

*The Silent War. The Decade of Blood. The Birth of the Brotherhood.*

> *"Thus began the Sundering of the Crown — a silent civil war fought in crypts and city shadows, where Brother hunted soldier, and soldier burned Brother."*

The Sundering of the Crown was the decade-long conflict that ended the [[The Sanctum Vigil|Sanctum Vigil]] as a unified institution and gave birth to the Vermeil Creed.

It was not a battle with a front line. It was a war fought in basements, in scriptorium fires, in the burning of records and the disappearance of scholars. For a decade, the land bled twice — from the Curse spreading across its surface, and from men who once wore the same crest killing each other in the dark.

---

## Cause

When Sanctum Vigil scholars discovered the **[[Tomb of the First Sin]]** — proof that [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant]] had murdered [[Akalaer]] and claimed forbidden power — they confronted the crown directly.

[[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|Alexandr's]] response was not negotiation. He declared them:
- **Heretics** — defilers of sacred law
- **Enemies of the realm** — who had sworn to protect it

The soldiers came. The burning began.

---

## The War

The Sundering was not documented publicly — Alexandr ensured that. What survives in the [[Crypt of Knowledge]] are fragments: accounts written by survivors, blood-marked pages from the field, reports of villages where Vigil members had hidden and were found.

**What is known:**
- Lasted approximately one decade
- Fought primarily in crypts, ruins, city underbellies — everywhere that was not visible
- The King's forces had numbers and resources; the defiant Brothers had knowledge and desperation
- Both sides lost figures of significance whose names the Brotherhood still records

**What Alexandr did during the Sundering:**
- Rewrote the scriptures — Akalaer's death became divine judgment in every official text
- Erased the Order's records from royal annals
- Outlawed their symbols
- Cursed their names publicly while hunting them privately

Only whispers remained of the defiant Brothers — of faceless hunters who fought creatures in the dark and vanished before dawn.

---

## The End — High Thalenor

When it became clear that open conflict was not survivable, the surviving defiant Brothers did not surrender.

They went to the ruins of **[[High Thalenor]]** — a site chosen specifically because it had no living king, no throne, no authority that could name them traitor or heretic. There, with some of the rulers of the shattered realms who had also been broken by the Curse and Alexandr's failures, they reforged their purpose.

The Vermeil Creed was named at High Thalenor. Its creed was carved into the stone beneath the hall.

---

## Significance

The Sundering is why the Brotherhood operates in secrecy. It is why they do not seek glory, crown, or public acknowledgement. It is why they were forced to become experts at working without institutional support — because institutional support was turned against them.

Every operational choice the Brotherhood makes — the distributed Creed structure, the hidden outposts, the blood-bound records that cannot be seized and burned, the [[Crypt of Knowledge]] buried underground — traces back to the lessons of the Sundering.

> [!quote] First Oath of the Brotherhood
> "We were born of a lie, but we live by truth."

The lie is Alexandr's. The birth is the Sundering. The Brotherhood is what survived it.

---

## In-Game Presence

The Sundering is historical — it happened centuries before the game's present. But its consequences are everywhere:

- The Brotherhood's secrecy and distributed structure
- The existence of the Crypt of Knowledge (built to preserve what was burned)
- The blood-oath system (built so no record could be taken and destroyed without the oath-holder knowing)
- The King's continued existence — sustained by the same forbidden power he gained by killing Akalaer
- The Curse itself — still spreading, still accelerating the [[Grand Clock]], seven centuries after the act that began it

The Sundering didn't end. It just became background.
