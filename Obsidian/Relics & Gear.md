# Relics & Gear

> [!quote] Fourth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "In blood, we learn. In death, we remember. In darkness, we preserve."

The Brotherhood does not trust clean hands. Every weapon they wield, every relic they carry, every sigil they etch was forged from or through the Curse itself. They did not choose this path because it was righteous — they chose it because it works.

> *"They wielded the magicks [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|Alexandr]] feared, the blood rites [[Akalaer]] fathered, the hex-steel forged from cursed veins beneath the earth. If it killed, protected, or preserved — it was sacred to them."*

---

## What Loot Is

Loot is the tangible record of what the Curse leaves behind when a Brotherhood Creed tears through it. Every item recovered from the field has passed through corruption — it is not clean, it is not safe, and it is exactly what the Brotherhood needs.

Loot is collected during **[[Combat#3.4 Breaths Between Floors|Breaths]]** and carried out only if at least one Brother survives. Items left in the field **despawn after 60 seconds.** In high-pressure missions, choosing what to collect becomes a decision in itself.

---

## Loot Types

### Blood Shards
*Currency of the Brotherhood*

Blood Shards are crystallised fragments of cursed essence — condensed corruption that the Brotherhood learned to safely handle and exchange. They are the most common drop from standard enemies and the primary currency at camp.

**Used for:**
- Ability upgrades at the workbench
- Equipment modification
- Restocking consumables
- Ritual components for relic verification

> [!note] Lore
> Blood Shards are what remains when a lesser curse-born is fully destroyed. The Brotherhood discovered centuries ago that the condensed essence could be safely handled if drawn out quickly — one of the many "methods armies feared" that the Brotherhood adopted after the [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering]].

---

### Consumables
*One-use field tools*

Consumables are single-use items carried into missions and used in the field — the Brotherhood's most practical knowledge, field-tested against things that should not exist.

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Healing Draught** | Restores a portion of a Brother's HP |
| **Hex Grenade** | Marks enemies in an area — synergises with Brotherhood Scholar abilities |
| **Sigil Scroll** | Deploys a temporary ward sigil; slows or damages enemies within its area |

> [!note] Lore — Sigil-Work
> The Brotherhood's sigils were originally developed by scholars who survived the [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering of the Crown]] — hunted by [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|the King's]] soldiers, they needed weapons that left no blade behind. Sigil-work became their answer: invisible, deniable, and lethal.

---

### Relic Fragments
*The pieces that matter*

Relic Fragments are rare drops from standard enemies — shards of larger artefacts broken by the Curse, scattered through cursed territory. They carry residual magickal signatures that the Brotherhood's magisters can trace.

At camp, Fragments are **forged** into complete Relics — a process requiring the right combination of Fragments and enough Blood Shards to fuel the ritual.

> [!note] Lore
> Every Relic Fragment that passes through the [[Blood Vault]] is logged in the recovering Brother's personal **Tomb** — its shape, its resonance, where it was found. Even fragments that never lead to a complete relic become part of the [[Crypt of Knowledge|Crypt's]] archive of the Curse's spread.

---

### Relics
*The reason the Brotherhood fights*

Relics are the game's primary gear layer — equipped items carried by Brothers that provide passive or active effects. They are the Brotherhood's most powerful tools and their most dangerous ones.

**Relics are the only items that drop from enemy variants.** Standard enemies never drop Relics. This keeps the risk-reward calculation clear: if you want gear, you fight the things that can kill you faster.

#### Drop Sources & Rates

| Enemy Tier | Rarity | Drop Chance | Gear Quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cursed** | Common | None | — |
| **Blighted** | Uncommon | ~25% | Low |
| **Corrupted** | Rare | ~10% | Mid |
| **Forsaken** | Legendary | ~5% | High / Best |

> [!warning] Design Intent
> A **Forsaken** enemy appearing mid-wave is an event. The Creed must decide in real time: push through it for the drop chance, or reposition and survive. Gear drops should feel like genuine discoveries — not routine rewards.

#### Relic Properties

Relics have **passive** and/or **active** effects. A Relic recovered by one Brother may be transferred to another at camp — the Brotherhood does not hoard knowledge, and it does not hoard power.

Relic effects are tied to their origin: a relic recovered from a Blighted enemy carries different corruption than one torn from a Forsaken. Magisters at camp assess each one and record its nature in the Crypt archive.

### Hex-Steel Gear
*Forged, not found*

Hex-steel is drawn from cursed veins beneath the earth — metal that has absorbed enough Curse energy to be re-worked into weapons and tools. Hex-steel blades cut through supernatural defences that standard steel cannot scratch.

Hex-steel forging becomes available at [[Brotherhood Progression#Rank 4 — Veilforged|Veilforged (Rank 4)]].

> [!note] Lore
> Hex-steel was one of the Brotherhood's most controversial early discoveries. [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|The King]] had banned all contact with cursed materials. The Brotherhood's answer was to forge weapons from them — an act the crown called desecration, and the Brotherhood called necessity.

---

### Ichor Cores
*Temporal material*

Ichor Cores are rare materials dropped exclusively by the **[[Combat#4.3 Hourglass Ichor (Special Spawn)|Hourglass Ichor]]** — and among the most valuable items a Creed can extract.

**Uses:**
- High-tier relic and hex-steel crafting
- **Slowing Grand Clock Pressure** — Cores processed at camp can be used in rituals that marginally reduce the rate at which the [[Grand Clock]]'s sands fall during subsequent missions
- Ritual components for the most advanced Brotherhood ceremonies

> [!note] Lore — The Grand Clock Connection
> The **[[Grand Clock]]**, forged by the god-smith [[Garin Loctove]] and raised on the shattered plains of **[[Kareth Vall]]**, is the countdown of the world itself. Its sands run faster with every act of corruption, every death, every sin — accelerated by [[Akalaer|Akalaer's]] murder and the Curse that followed.
>
> The Hourglass Ichor is condensed temporal corruption — a direct manifestation of the Clock's bleed into reality. Destroying it and extracting its Core is the Brotherhood's most direct method of reclaiming ground in the war against time.
>
> This is why the Brotherhood fights. Not for kingdoms or glory — to **slow the fall of the sands.**

## Relic Recovery Missions

The [[Combat#Relic Recovery|Relic Recovery]] mission type is named exactly for this system. These are not generic loot runs — they are **specific retrieval operations** commissioned by the Brotherhood to recover named artefacts lost to the Curse.

**Examples from active mission logs:**
- **Recover the Sealed Tome** — a blood-bound Brotherhood text lost when a field Creed was wiped; contents unknown, provenance critical. If the blood signature matches the Scrolls of Mortality, everything inside is preserved to the [[Crypt of Knowledge]].
- **Retrieve the Warden's Sigil** — a relic belonging to a fallen Brother of significant rank. The Sigil's bonding to that Brother's blood may hold information the Crypt cannot afford to lose.

In both cases: the item is real, its location approximate, and a **Relic Warden** will defend it. The Warden is not a random spawn — it is the Curse's answer to a Brotherhood recovery operation. The Curse knows what the Brotherhood wants.

## Camp — Loot Processing

When surviving Brothers extract and return to camp, loot is processed:

1. **Blood Shards** — added directly to the Creed's reserves
2. **Consumables** — stocked into the field kit
3. **Relic Fragments** — logged in the recovering Brother's [[Blood Vault|Tomb entry]], stored in the relic vault pending forging
4. **Relics** — assessed by magisters (available from [[Brotherhood Progression#Rank 5 — Eclipsed Sigil|Rank 5]]+), logged, equipped or vaulted
5. **Ichor Cores** — secured separately; priority material, allocated by Creed rank

> [!info]
> From [[Brotherhood Progression#Rank 5 — Eclipsed Sigil|Rank 5 (Eclipsed Sigil)]] onward, recovered Relics are automatically documented in the [[Crypt of Knowledge]] archive — their properties added to the Brotherhood's growing catalogue of the Curse's artefacts.

## The Brotherhood's Relationship with Its Gear

The Brotherhood does not revere its weapons. It does not name its blades for glory or commission monuments to its relics. Gear is **functional truth** — the physical evidence of what the Curse is and what can be done with it.

When a Brother dies, the gear they carried is recovered with their [[Blood Vault#Tombs of Blood and Ink|Tomb]] if possible. Relics of significance are re-assessed at the Crypt. If the relic was bonded to a specific Brother's blood (as some high-tier Forsaken drops are), the magister determines whether it can be re-bonded or must be archived permanently.

> [!quote] Eighth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "Knowledge not born of oath is rot."

The same applies to gear. A relic held by an unbound hand is not a weapon — it is a hazard.
