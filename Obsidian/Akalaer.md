# Akalaer

*The Arch-Warden. The Shadowed Father. The First Sin's Other Half.*

> *"It began with a lie."*

Akalaer was the **Arch-Warden** — the shadowed father of necromancy, and the keeper of the balance between life and death in the world. He was not a king, not a god, but something that sat between them: a being whose existence was structural, not personal.

When he died, the world did not mourn him. It broke.

---

## What He Was

Akalaer is described in Brotherhood records as the **Arch-Warden and shadowed father of necromancy** — a title that has been misread by outsiders as evidence of malevolence. The Brotherhood's interpretation is different.

He did not wield necromancy to conquer. He wielded it to **maintain balance**. Life and death are not opposites — they are a cycle, and Akalaer was the thing that kept the cycle turning. The blood rites the Brotherhood uses, the knowledge of death-magick that powers their most effective tools — these trace their lineage back to what Akalaer understood about the nature of existence.

> [!note]
> The Brotherhood calls him the "shadowed father" of necromancy not as condemnation, but as acknowledgement. The magick they use to fight the Curse is, in part, the magick he fathered. They use what he understood — and they do it in his memory, whether they say so or not.

---

## His Death

Akalaer was struck down by **[[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant]]** — not in battle, not in self-defence, but in **deliberate murder** driven by the King's zealous bloodlust and desire for forbidden power.

The moment Akalaer's heart stopped:
- The ground turned cold
- The stars dimmed
- The first breath of the **Curse** crept across the kingdoms
- The **[[Grand Clock]]** trembled — storms of golden dust rose from [[Kareth Vall]], and the chimes grew uneven

The world's balance had been held, in part, by Akalaer's continued existence. His death did not just remove him — it removed something structural from reality itself.

> [!quote]
> *"The very instant Akalaer's heart stopped, the ground turned cold, the stars dimmed, and the first breath of the Curse crept across the kingdoms."*

---

## The Cover-Up

[[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|Alexandr]] named Akalaer's death a **divine punishment** — a trial sent by the gods for humanity's pride. He rewrote the scriptures. He commanded temples to call it judgment.

The truth he buried: his own hand had torn the balance apart. And in killing Akalaer, he had claimed forbidden power — the very magicks that now poisoned the earth.

This truth was uncovered by the scholars of the [[The Sanctum Vigil|Sanctum Vigil]] — the original order Alexandr had formed in the wake of the Curse. The proof they found in the **[[Tomb of the First Sin]]** ended their loyalty to the crown and began the [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering]].

---

## His Return

The Brotherhood did not abandon their oaths to the Sanctum Vigil simply to escape the King. They abandoned them to take on something heavier: **the burden of containing the curse, preserving the truth, and preparing for the day Akalaer would return.**

Whether this refers to a literal resurrection, a spiritual return, the resolution of the imbalance his death caused, or something else entirely — the Brotherhood does not state clearly. The oldest tomes in the [[Crypt of Knowledge]] use this phrase without elaboration.

> [!info] Open Question
> *"Prepare for the day Akalaer would return"* — what does this mean in game terms? Possible interpretations:
> - The endgame condition: reversing the Curse requires restoring the balance Akalaer represented
> - The ultimate mission: the Grand Clock stops falling when the world's balance is restored
> - A literal resurrection arc — the Brotherhood seeks to bring Akalaer back
> - A philosophical return — the principles Akalaer embodied must be re-established, not the person

---

## Legacy in the Brotherhood

Akalaer's influence on the Brotherhood is total, even if unspoken.

- The **blood rites** they perform trace to his lineage
- The **Rite of Names** — the binding of a Brother's essence to the Brotherhood's record — is necromantic in structure, even if the Brotherhood does not frame it that way
- The **[[Blood Vault]]**'s capacity to "know" a Brother — their pulse, their magick, their fate — is a function of the same magick Akalaer understood
- Every enemy that rises from the Curse is, in some sense, a consequence of his absence

The Brotherhood does not worship Akalaer. But they operate in the world his death created, with tools his knowledge made possible, toward a purpose his murder made necessary.
