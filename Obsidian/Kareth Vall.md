# Kareth Vall

*The Shattered Plains. The Clock's Ground. The Last Stand.*

> *"It towers upon the shattered plains of Kareth Vall, a relic from before memory, a structure so vast it scrapes the heavens."*

Kareth Vall is the location of the **[[Grand Clock]]** — the most significant geographical site in the known world, and one of the most dangerous.

Once a landscape of something unknown — its original form predates surviving records — the plains of Kareth Vall are now **shattered**: cracked earth, strange geological formations, a terrain that looks as though something immense landed on it or erupted from within it. The Brotherhood's oldest tomes disagree on what caused it. Several suggest the act of placing the Clock there was itself the cause.

---

## The Grand Clock's Presence

The [[Grand Clock]] dominates Kareth Vall entirely. Its twin glass chambers are visible from leagues away — from the right elevation, reportedly from neighbouring kingdoms. Its chime carries across the plains in a low resonance that some visitors describe as felt in the chest rather than heard.

The golden sand that bleeds from the upper chamber to the lower casts a faint amber light across the plains at certain times of day — dawn and dusk, when the sun catches the glass at low angles.

After [[Akalaer|Akalaer's]] death, witnesses at Kareth Vall reported:
- Storms of golden dust rising from the Clock's base
- The chimes growing uneven — faltering, then resuming faster
- The ground near the Clock cracking further, as though even the earth reacted

## Brotherhood Significance

The **[[Brotherhood Progression#The Council of Shadows|Council of Shadows]]** convenes at Kareth Vall once per generation — the most senior Creeds gathering beneath the Grand Clock to guide the unseen war.

The Clock's shadow falls over the proceedings. Its chime marks the opening and closing of each session.

This is not ceremony for its own sake. The Brotherhood meets beneath the Clock to remind themselves — physically, bodily, with the sound of it in their bones — what they are working against.

## In the Field — "Last Stand at Kareth Vall"

**Last Stand at Kareth Vall** is one of the named [[Combat#Chaos|Chaos mission]] scenarios — the highest-risk mission type, recommended only for established Creeds.

A mission set at Kareth Vall means the Creed is fighting in the shadow of the Grand Clock itself — at a site where the temporal bleed is not a mission modifier, it is the environment. Grand Clock Pressure here does not just accelerate wave timing; the Clock's proximity warps local time in ways that make the [[Combat#4.3 Hourglass Ichor (Special Spawn)|Hourglass Ichor]] appear more frequently and in greater numbers.

> [!warning]
> Kareth Vall missions are tuned for high-rank Creeds. The site's proximity to the Clock means enemy spawn rates, Hourglass Ichor frequency, and Grand Clock Pressure levels are at their highest outside of directly Clock-adjacent rifts.

The naming of the Chaos phase **"Grand Toll"** in the wave structure is a direct reference to the Clock's final chime — and missions at Kareth Vall begin that much closer to it.

## Atmosphere Notes

For future art and environment reference:
- **Terrain:** Shattered plains — cracked earth, fractured rock formations, nothing grows cleanly
- **Light:** The Clock's glass chambers cast amber light at dawn/dusk; golden dust visible near the base
- **Sound:** The Clock's resonant chime underlies everything — it does not stop
- **Scale:** The Clock itself dominates the skyline; everything on the plains exists in its visual shadow
- **Danger gradient:** The closer to the Clock's base, the higher the temporal bleed — enemy behaviour is altered near the structure itself
