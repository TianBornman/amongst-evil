# Alexandr the Iron Tyrant

*The King. The Liar. The Undoing.*

> *"He was not the world's savior — he was its undoing."*

Alexandr — known in Brotherhood records as **the Iron Tyrant** — was the king who murdered [[Akalaer]], unleashed the Curse upon the world, and then built an empire of lies to cover what he had done.

He is the reason the Vermeil Creed exists. He is the First Sin.

---

## The Murder of Akalaer

Driven by **zealous bloodlust** and a hunger for forbidden power, Alexandr struck down [[Akalaer]] — the Arch-Warden, the being whose existence helped maintain the balance between life and death.

The moment Akalaer fell, the Curse spread. The [[Grand Clock]] trembled. The world broke.

Alexandr knew. He was there. His hand drove the blade. And in the act of killing Akalaer, he **claimed the forbidden power** he had coveted — the very magicks that would go on to poison the earth.

> [!quote]
> *"His own hand, guided by his own zealous bloodlust, had torn the balance apart."*

---

## The Lie

Rather than confess, Alexandr named the Curse a **divine punishment** — a trial from the gods for humanity's pride. He positioned himself as the world's only hope of surviving it.

To execute this lie, he:
- Formed the **[[The Sanctum Vigil|Sanctum Vigil]]** — a new holy order of knights, magi, priests, and scholars sworn to fight the Curse under his banner
- Rewrote the scriptures to name Akalaer's death as divine judgment
- Erased the original order's records when they uncovered the truth
- Declared the Brotherhood heretics and traitors when they defied him
- Hunted Brotherhood grandmasters and erased them from history

The lie spread faster than the truth could follow.

---

## His Continued Existence

Seven centuries after Akalaer's fall, the Brotherhood's records note that **Alexandr still rules from his decaying throne** — sustained by magick he publicly swore to destroy.

He did not age. He did not die. He used the forbidden power he claimed from Akalaer's murder to sustain himself — the same power that fuels the Curse, that poisons the earth, that runs the [[Grand Clock]] faster.

His kingdom endures. Not by his power — but by the Brotherhood's unseen work beneath it, fighting the consequences of what he started, keeping the people alive despite the man their ancestors swore to serve.

> [!quote]
> *"The decrepit King still ruled from his decaying throne — sustained by magick he swore to kill — his kingdom endured not by his power, but by the unseen hands that fought beneath it."*

---

## The Sundering

When the scholars of the Sanctum Vigil uncovered the **[[Tomb of the First Sin]]** — proof that Alexandr had murdered Akalaer and claimed forbidden power — they confronted the crown.

Rather than confession, they were met with **steel and fire**.

Alexandr declared them heretics, defilers of sacred law, enemies of the realm. The [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering of the Crown]] began: a decade-long silent civil war fought in crypts and city shadows. Brother hunted soldier. Soldier burned Brother.

The Brothers who survived retreated to the ruins of [[High Thalenor]], reforged their purpose, and became the Vermeil Creed — an order that owes nothing to the man who made them necessary.

---

## Public Perception

To the common folk, Alexandr is still the King — the ruler who formed the order that fought the Curse, who kept the world from collapsing. The Brotherhood's version of history is not available to them. The scriptures say what Alexandr made them say.

Some royal messengers — when darkness rises and monsters prowl — disappear into the night to seek Brotherhood help. Even the servants of the crown that publicly condemns them.

---

## In-Game Presence

Alexandr is not a mission target — not yet. He is **the context behind every mission**, the reason every curse-born exists, the man whose theft of forbidden power set the Grand Clock running faster.

Whether he eventually becomes a narrative endpoint, a final boss, or a looming presence that the player never directly confronts is an open design question.

> [!info] Open Question
> Alexandr's sustained existence through forbidden magick — the same magick that fuels the Curse — suggests a mechanical connection. Is killing him the way to slow the Clock? Or would his death accelerate it? Does his life somehow contain part of what Akalaer was?
