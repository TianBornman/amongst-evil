# The Sanctum Vigil

*The First Order. The Lie Made Holy. The Brotherhood Before It Was.*

> *"To calm his people and preserve his throne, the King forged a new order in the image of unity: The Sanctum Vigil."*

The Sanctum Vigil was the original holy order formed by **[[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant]]** in the aftermath of [[Akalaer|Akalaer's]] death — not to fight the Curse, but to **control the story of it**.

It was the institution the Vermeil Creed grew out of, turned against, and ultimately replaced.

---

## Formation

When [[Akalaer]] fell and the Curse began spreading, [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|Alexandr]] needed an explanation and an army. The Sanctum Vigil served as both.

**Members:** Knights, magi, priests, and scholars
**Stated purpose:** Defend the living from horrors born of the Curse
**Actual purpose:** Give the King's lie institutional legitimacy — a holy order fighting the "divine punishment" he had named the Curse

For a time, the Vigil served genuinely. They:
- Fought the nightmare-spawned beasts
- Purified villages
- Brought hope back to the broken

They were real warriors doing real work. The lie that built them did not make the work less real.

---

## The Discovery

The Vigil's scholars were too thorough. The deeper they delved into the Curse's origin, the darker the truth became.

In unassuming ruins, they uncovered the **[[Tomb of the First Sin]]** — relics and writings proving beyond doubt that:
- The King had not only slain Akalaer — he had **claimed forbidden power** from the act
- He was using the very magicks that now poisoned the earth
- He was not the world's savior — he was **its undoing**

The scholars brought this proof to the crown. They expected confession. Or at minimum, acknowledgement.

---

## The Confrontation and Sundering

They were met with **steel and fire**.

Alexandr declared the scholars heretics, defilers of sacred law, enemies of the realm they had sworn to protect. The [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering of the Crown]] began immediately — a silent civil war in crypts and city shadows.

The Vigil members who survived and refused to recant:
- Burned their royal banners
- Abandoned the crown entirely
- Retreated to the ruins of [[High Thalenor]]
- Reforged their purpose into what would become the **Vermeil Creed**

Those who stayed in the Vigil — whether through fear, ambition, or genuine belief in the King's revised version — became instruments of the cover-up.

---

## What Remained

The Sanctum Vigil continued to exist after the Sundering — hollowed out, its original purpose replaced by whatever Alexandr needed from it. Its scholars were replaced with ones who would not ask dangerous questions. Its records were rewritten. Its mission continued, but under a lie it could no longer question.

Whether the Vigil still exists in the game's present — seven centuries later — is an open question. If it does, its relationship to the Vermeil Creed would be complex: they share the same origin, the same early mission, and a complete split in the truth they were willing to accept.

> [!info] Open Question
> The Sanctum Vigil's current status — active, dissolved, corrupted by the King's magick, or transformed into something else over centuries — has not been established. This is worth defining as the world expands.
