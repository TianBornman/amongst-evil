# Tomb of the First Sin

*The Proof. The Trigger. The Reason.*

> *"In unassuming ruins they uncovered the Tomb of the First Sin — relics and writings that proved beyond doubt that the King had not only slain Akalaer but had claimed forbidden power."*

The Tomb of the First Sin is the site — and the artefact collection — that ended the [[The Sanctum Vigil|Sanctum Vigil's]] loyalty to the crown and triggered the [[The Sundering of the Crown|Sundering of the Crown]].

It is the physical proof that [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant]] murdered [[Akalaer]], claimed forbidden power from the act, and then lied to the world about all of it.

---

## Discovery

Vigil scholars, tracing the Curse's origin through methodical research, found the Tomb in **unassuming ruins** — a location that gave no outward sign of its significance. The Brotherhood records do not specify exactly where.

Inside:
- **Relics** from the act of Akalaer's murder — physical objects bearing the resonance of what happened
- **Writings** — documents, possibly Alexandr's own or those of witnesses, recording the truth
- Evidence of the forbidden power claimed — the same magicks that now poisoned the earth, traceable to the King's hand

The combination constituted proof that could not be denied, misread, or argued away.

---

## The Contents

The Tomb of the First Sin currently resides in the **[[Crypt of Knowledge]]** — at least, copies of what was recovered do. The original relics are held in sealed reliquaries accessible only to [[Brotherhood Progression#Rank 8 — Obsidian Hand|Rank 8+]] Creeds and Brotherhood magisters.

The specific contents of the Tomb have never been fully documented in open Brotherhood records. What is known is what they proved — not what they look like or how the proof worked.

> [!info] Open Question
> The Tomb's exact location in the ruins, the nature of the relics (what are they physically?), and the content of the writings (whose hand? what language? how were they preserved?) are all undefined. Worth establishing — this is the inciting artefact of the entire Brotherhood's existence.

---

## Significance

The Tomb of the First Sin is the Brotherhood's founding document — not in the sense of a charter or creed, but in the sense of *the reason a charter was necessary*. Before the Tomb was discovered, the Vigil served the crown in good faith. After it was discovered, that faith was impossible.

Everything that followed — the confrontation with the crown, the Sundering, the flight to [[High Thalenor]], the founding creed carved in stone — flows directly from what the scholars found in those ruins.

The Brotherhood preserves it not as a trophy. They preserve it as **evidence** — the proof that their defiance was justified, that the King's lie was real, and that the truth they protect is worth protecting.

> [!quote] First Oath of the Brotherhood
> "We were born of a lie, but we live by truth."

The lie has a physical location. The Tomb of the First Sin is it.
