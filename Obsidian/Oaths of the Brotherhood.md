# Oaths of the Brotherhood

> [!info] The Name
> **Vermeil** is an archaic word for *blood-red* — a colour the Brotherhood wears in everything but cloth: in the ink of their Tombs, in the ritual of the Rite of Names, in the price paid for every truth they preserve.
> **Creed** is not a title of rank or lineage. It is an oath-word. The Brotherhood is named for what it swears, not what it rules.

Every Brother swears all fifteen oaths on initiation — during or immediately after the **[[Blood Vault#The Rite of Names|Rite of Names]]**. The oaths are not aspirations. They are binding conditions of the blood-oath that ties a Brother to the [[Blood Vault]].

Swearing falsely is not punished by the Brotherhood. The Vault punishes it — in ways the Brotherhood has documented but does not discuss openly.

---

## The Fifteen Oaths

> [!quote] First Oath
> "We were born of a lie, but we live by truth."

*The founding oath — spoken first at [[High Thalenor]] and repeated at every initiation since. References [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|Alexandr's]] original lie that covered [[Akalaer|Akalaer's]] murder.*

---

> [!quote] Second Oath
> "When gods die, men inherit their curses."

*The theological statement of the Brotherhood's world. [[Akalaer]] was not a god — but his death functioned like one. The Curse is what men inherit when the beings that maintained the world's balance are removed.*

---

> [!quote] Third Oath
> "We are the hand that slows the fall of the hourglass."

*The mission statement. The [[Grand Clock]] at [[Kareth Vall]] runs faster since [[Akalaer|Akalaer's]] death — the Brotherhood's purpose is to slow it. Not stop it. Slow it.*

---

> [!quote] Fourth Oath
> "In blood, we learn. In death, we remember. In darkness, we preserve."

*The methodology. Blood = the [[Blood Vault]] and the oath-binding system. Death = the Tombs of fallen Brothers and what they carry to the [[Crypt of Knowledge]]. Darkness = the work done without acknowledgement.*

---

> [!quote] Fifth Oath
> "We seek no crown, no song, no praise. We serve the world that forgot us."

*The ethos of anonymity. The Brotherhood works in secret not just from necessity — it is doctrine. Recognition corrupts. The King proved that.*

---

> [!quote] Sixth Oath
> "Only blood remembers what the tongue must forget."

*The oath of the [[Blood Vault]]. What a Brother knows about themselves, their mission, their weaknesses — the Vault holds it. The tongue forgets, on purpose, what the record preserves.*

---

> [!quote] Seventh Oath
> "The living serve. The dead teach. The blood remembers."

*The Brotherhood's relationship with death. Surviving Brothers serve the Creed actively. Fallen Brothers serve through their Tombs — the knowledge they left behind. The blood (the Vault's record) preserves both.*

---

> [!quote] Eighth Oath
> "Knowledge not born of oath is rot."

*The standard for the [[Crypt of Knowledge]]. Knowledge that comes from someone not bound by the Brotherhood's oath system — someone whose blood is not in the Scrolls of Mortality — cannot be trusted, verified, or preserved. It is burned.*

---

> [!quote] Ninth Oath
> "The curse feeds on pride — we feed it humility and steel."

*The practical combat philosophy. The Curse is not defeated by glory-seeking. It is managed through discipline, knowledge, and tools that work — regardless of how those tools were made.*

---

> [!quote] Tenth Oath
> "We wield the dark not to rule, but to restrain it."

*The ethical boundary. The Brotherhood uses forbidden magick, blood rites, and curse-touched material — but the purpose is restraint, not dominion. The moment a Brother uses the dark to rule rather than restrain, the Oath is broken.*

---

> [!quote] Eleventh Oath
> "Our hands are stained so others may stay clean."

*The Brotherhood's public contract with a world that doesn't know it exists. They absorb the moral cost of using methods armies fear and kings forbid, so ordinary people don't have to.*

---

> [!quote] Twelfth Oath
> "Every fallen Brother marks a page in eternity."

*The death oath. Falling is not failure — it is contribution. Every Brother who dies in the field leaves their Tomb, their knowledge, their entry in the [[Blood Vault]] sealed as a final snapshot. The page is permanent.*

---

> [!quote] Thirteenth Oath
> "Truth sleeps in silence; we are its waking blade."

*The archival mission stated as action. The truth of [[Alexandr the Iron Tyrant|the King's]] sin, of [[Akalaer|Akalaer's]] death, of the Curse's origin — these truths are buried and sleeping. The Brotherhood is the thing that keeps them from disappearing entirely.*

---

> [!quote] Fourteenth Oath
> "The hour falls for all — we only slow its hand."

*The humility of the mission. The Brotherhood does not claim to be able to stop the [[Grand Clock]]. They claim only to slow it — to buy the time needed to understand what is happening and, perhaps, how to end it.*

---

> [!quote] Fifteenth Oath
> "For as long as one Brother stands, the world has not yet fallen."

*The final oath — the one that matters when everything else is gone. A single surviving Brother means the Brotherhood continues. The mission continues. The world continues. This oath is why a Wipe (all Brothers dead) is the only true failure.*

## The Weight of the Oaths

Brothers swear these oaths with their blood in the Scrolls of Mortality. The [[Blood Vault]] knows whether the oaths hold. The Brotherhood does not publicly define the consequences of oath-breaking — that knowledge is itself restricted to high-rank Creeds.

What is recorded: Brothers who broke specific oaths were removed from the Blood Vault's active rolls before their deaths. Their entries appear in the archive but without the Fallen marker — a category the Brotherhood calls the **Unrecorded**, about which almost nothing has been written.
