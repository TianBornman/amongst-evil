# The Blood Vault

> [!quote] Sixth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "Only blood remembers what the tongue must forget."

The Blood Vault is not a graveyard. It is not a trophy wall. It is a **living chronicle** — the Brotherhood's eternal memory, etched in blood and bound by magick older than any kingdom.

Every Brother who has ever taken the [[#The Rite of Names|Rite of Names]] lives inside the Vault. Living and dead alike. The entries do not change category when a Brother falls — they simply stop updating.

---

## Location

The Blood Vault is housed deep beneath the **[[Crypt of Knowledge]]**, in chambers that predate the Brotherhood itself. Iron lanterns burn with **blue flame** that does not consume — lit, according to legend, on the day of the Brotherhood's founding and never extinguished since. The air carries the mineral tang of old blood and cold stone.

Every drop of blood ever shed in service to the Brotherhood still **hums with magickal resonance** in these walls. Initiates entering for the first time often describe the sensation as being watched — not by eyes, but by memory itself.

---

## The Rite of Names

Initiation into the Vermeil Creed is not an application. The Brotherhood does not recruit — **it recognises.**

Those chosen are:
- Survivors of cursed lands, carrying wounds the world cannot explain
- Scholars who uncovered truths no sane mind should hold
- Warriors who killed what should not die

When one is brought before the Blood Vault, they are not yet a Brother. They are *mortal ambition wrapped in trembling flesh.*

### The Ritual

The initiate descends beneath the [[Crypt of Knowledge]] to stand before the **Scrolls of Mortality** — a living tome that binds essence to the Brotherhood's eternal record.

There, they write their name. The ink is their blood.

Once the name bleeds into the parchment, the Vault **whispers it** — etching it into the Brotherhood's living memory. From that moment:

> *"You are marked. The Brotherhood bleeds as you bleed."*

The Vault now knows them: their **pulse, their magick, their fate.** A Brother cannot hide from the Vault. A Brother cannot lie to it. The record is absolute.

---

## The Loss of Name

Few Brothers survive long enough to earn an alias. For most, the name written in the Scrolls becomes their only gravestone.

But for those who endure — who survive the curse-born horrors, the haunted ruins, the things that crawl from the dark — something changes. The Brotherhood **strips them of their mortal name.**

Keeping one's old name ties the soul too closely to the world of men. The Brotherhood belongs to no king, no faith, no past.

Each survivor is renamed in ceremony by their **Creed's Keeper** — their new title reflecting what they have become:

| Alias | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Warden** | Those who protect the living from the dark |
| **The Hollow** | Those who sacrifice all humanity to study death |
| **Ashblade** | Those who burn everything in pursuit of purity |
| **Seraph Null** | Those who have walked too close to godhood |

The new name is written in blood **over** the old one in the Scrolls. From that moment, the mortal name fades — unreadable, erased by the Vault's magick. The person they were is gone. What remains belongs to the Brotherhood.

---

## What the Vault Tracks

Every Brother's entry is a living record. It grows with every mission, every kill, every relic recovered.

**Active Brothers** — entry updates in real time:
- Original name *(sealed behind their alias after naming ceremony)*
- Alias / Brotherhood title
- Current rank on the [[Brotherhood Progression#The Spiral of the Veil|Spiral of the Veil]]
- Missions survived
- Kill count
- Relics recovered and documented
- Status: **Active**
- Link to their personal **[[#Tombs of Blood and Ink|Tomb]]**

**Fallen Brothers** — entry becomes a final snapshot:
- Everything above, frozen at the moment of death
- Cause of death
- Mission they died on
- Enemy or force that killed them
- Status: **Fallen**
- Tomb sealed and preserved as-is

> [!note]
> Entries cannot be deleted or overwritten. Every Brother who ever served is permanently on record — the Vault does not forget, and it does not forgive omission.

---

## Tombs of Blood and Ink

Every Brother keeps a **Personal Tomb** — a thick tome bound in the skin of the fallen and written entirely in their own blood. These are both journal and confession:

- Sketches of monsters, their weaknesses, their behaviours
- Relic discoveries and theories on their origin
- Personal accounts of hunts, near-deaths, and what was learned from both
- Theories on the Curse's nature and the Grand Clock's acceleration
- Final confessions

The Tomb is a Brother's contribution to the [[Crypt of Knowledge]]. It is their legacy — the knowledge they earned with their blood, offered to those who come after.

### When a Brother Dies

If a Brother falls in the field, their Creed is expected to **recover the Tomb** and carry it out. This is not optional — it is sacred obligation.

Upon return to the [[Crypt of Knowledge]], **magisters** verify the Tomb through ritual magick:

1. Blood from the Tomb's pages is tested against the name written in the Scrolls of Mortality
2. If the blood matches — if the Tomb belongs to a soul bound by the Vault — the knowledge within is **preserved, copied, and added to the eternal record**
3. If the blood does not match — if the writer was never bound by oath — the book is **burned**, and the ashes cast to the Grand Clock's wind

> [!quote] Eighth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "Knowledge not born of oath is rot."

## The Vault in Camp

The Blood Vault is accessible from camp at all times. It functions as the Brotherhood's **living roster** — a record of who is active, who has fallen, and what they have done.

Over time, it becomes something else without announcement: the same names, the same record, but some of them no longer changing. New entries appear. Old entries go still. The Vault does not mourn — it simply **remembers**.

> [!tip] Design Intent
> The Blood Vault should feel like a Brotherhood roster that quietly becomes a memorial. The transition from "roster" to "memorial" should happen naturally as Brothers fall — never announced, never dramatised. The player should feel it before they name it.

### Sealed Entries

A Brother who has fallen has their entry **sealed**. Their Creed slot is vacated. They cannot be selected for missions. Their alias remains in the Vault, their Tomb preserved in the Crypt — a permanent page in the Brotherhood's growing scripture.

> [!quote] Twelfth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "Every fallen Brother marks a page in eternity."

## Lore Connections

- The Rite of Names is what makes a Brother eligible to appear in the Vault — and therefore eligible to be selected for a [[Combat|Creed]]
- The Vault's records feed the [[Crypt of Knowledge]] — recovered Tombs add to the archive
- Rank advancement through the [[Brotherhood Progression#The Spiral of the Veil|Spiral of the Veil]] is reflected in each Brother's entry
- Relics recovered by a Brother are logged in their entry and documented in their Tomb — see [[Relics & Gear]]
- The blue-flame lanterns of the Vault chamber are the same ghostlight that burns in the Crypt's halls
