# Brotherhood Progression

> [!quote] Third Oath of the Brotherhood
> "We are the hand that slows the fall of the hourglass."

The Brotherhood does not measure progress in trophies or titles. It measures it in **deeds whispered and secrets kept** — in the weight of knowledge carried, the horrors survived, and the truth preserved at cost.

The system that tracks this is called **The Spiral of the Veil**.

---

## The Spiral of the Veil

The Spiral is an unbroken circle of symbols representing the slow approach toward the inner truth of the Brotherhood — and of the Curse itself. A Creed's rank on the Spiral reflects their standing within the wider Brotherhood network: what missions they are trusted with, what knowledge they may access, and what resources the Brotherhood extends to them.

**A Creed rises through deeds — not time.**

> [!note] In-Game Tie
> The **Threat Rating** on each [[Combat#2.2 Mission Briefing|mission briefing]] (I through X) maps directly to the Spiral's ten ranks. A Threat I mission is tuned for *Whispering Ashes* Creeds. A Threat X mission is the domain of the *Clockless*. Attempting missions beyond your Creed's rank is not forbidden — it is simply likely fatal.

---

### Rank 1 — Whispering Ashes
*Status: Unrecognised / Aspirants*

Your Creed exists only as rumour. You do not know if the Brotherhood is real or a myth. Survival and small deeds — clearing cursed ruins, following faint leads — are all that mark your beginning.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating I
**Resources:** None — the Brotherhood does not yet acknowledge you
**Camp:** Bare bones — a fire, a few blades, the gear you carried in

> *"Smoke in the wind, whispers that may never be heard."*

---

### Rank 2 — Shrouded Vow
*Status: Initiates / Recognised*

The Brotherhood has noted you in hidden records. Limited guidance and resources are provided. You begin to gather relics, uncover minor secrets, and prove your resolve. The [[Blood Vault]] now holds your names — the **Rite of Names** has been performed, your blood is in the Scrolls of Mortality.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating II
**Resources:** Basic supply access; limited Brotherhood contacts
**Camp:** A proper shelter. Relic storage. The Blood Vault terminal becomes active.

> *"Our names are known, but our faces remain unseen."*

---

### Rank 3 — Iron Phalanx
*Status: Bound to the Purpose*

Your Creed acts as a single unit. You maintain hidden outposts, train new aspirants, and take on dangerous missions. Your reputation quietly spreads across the Brotherhood network. This is where most Creeds who survive their first real encounters settle.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating III
**Resources:** Basic Brotherhood weapon modifications; consumable restocking
**Camp:** Fortified outpost. Training grounds. Ability upgrade workbench.

> *"Bound by steel and oath, we move as one purpose."*

---

### Rank 4 — Veilforged
*Status: Trusted Arm of the Brotherhood*

Your Creed gains access to **forbidden artefacts** — items that other Creeds cannot safely handle. High-risk rituals. The trust to strike at threats even Brotherhood veterans fear. Brothers at this rank may begin earning their **aliases** — their mortal names stripped by their Creed's Keeper and replaced with what they have become.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating IV
**Resources:** Forbidden relic access; hex-steel weapon forging; ritual components
**Camp:** Relic vault unlocked. Ritual chamber. Recruitment board active.

> *"Forged in shadow, we shape the unseen war."*

---

### Rank 5 — Eclipsed Sigil
*Status: Revered / Feared*

Your Creed is a name whispered in awe across the Brotherhood's hidden network. You guard secrets most Creeds cannot touch. Your guidance influences operations across multiple regions. Relics recovered at this rank begin to be automatically catalogued in the [[Crypt of Knowledge]].

**Mission access:** Threat Rating V
**Resources:** High-tier relic crafting; Brotherhood intelligence network; Crypt of Knowledge reading access
**Camp:** Expanded archive. Map room. Senior Brothers recover from injury faster.

> *"Our sigil casts shadows long enough to hide the world."*

---

### Rank 6 — Nightbound Covenant
*Status: Elite Operatives*

You control multiple sub-Creeds and oversee strategic operations. Missions are now intertwined with **slowing the [[Grand Clock]]** and containing major curse outbreaks across regions. Your Creed begins to leave a permanent mark on the Brotherhood's history.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating VI
**Resources:** Grand Clock monitoring data; Ichor Core priority allocation; sub-Creed support on some missions
**Camp:** Grand Clock observation post. Ichor Core refinery. Brotherhood emissary visits.

> *"We walk where the night binds the world."*

---

### Rank 7 — Shadowed Archive
*Status: Keepers of Forbidden Knowledge*

Your Creed manages access to the **[[Crypt of Knowledge]]** — curating blood-bound tomes, safeguarding truths the world cannot know. Other Creeds consult you on matters of magick, history, and strategy. The knowledge recovered by your fallen Brothers carries more weight here than at any other rank — their Tombs are treated as primary sources.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating VII
**Resources:** Full Crypt access; recovered Tomb priority processing; commission research from Brotherhood magisters
**Camp:** Crypt annex. Magister workbench. Fallen Brothers' Tombs displayed in the hall.

> *"We hold the truths that the world dares not remember."*

---

### Rank 8 — Obsidian Hand
*Status: Commanders of the Hidden Wars*

Your Creed executes the most dangerous and sacred missions — the ones that do not appear in any briefing, that leave no record but in blood. The **Council of Shadows** takes note of your actions. Smaller Creeds look to you for leadership; your decisions ripple outward through the network.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating VIII
**Resources:** Council-sanctioned mission access; first pick of Forsaken-tier loot; Brotherhood fortress access
**Camp:** Command chamber. Council communiqué board. Elder Brothers available as advisors.

> *"Our grip unseen, yet the world bends beneath it."*

---

### Rank 9 — Prime Obsidian
*Status: The Heart of the Shadows*

The Creed at the centre of the Brotherhood. Unparalleled access to knowledge, relics, and strategic authority. Decisions made at this rank can **shift the fate of nations and the flow of the Grand Clock itself.** Few have stood here. Fewer have remained standing.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating IX
**Resources:** Unrestricted Brotherhood resources; Grandmaster audience; Grand Clock direct observation rights at [[Kareth Vall]]
**Camp:** Grandmaster's sanctum access. All systems fully unlocked.

> *"The clock does not strike without our hand guiding its hour."*

---

### Rank 10 — Clockless
*Status: Beyond the Veil / Mythic*

Legends say the Clockless exist outside time itself. Their influence shapes events unseen by all. They are said to walk between the ticks of the **[[Grand Clock]]**, bound to the universe, answerable to nothing but the hourglass.

No living Creed has confirmed this rank exists. The Brotherhood's records reference it only in the oldest blood-bound tomes — in handwriting no magister has been able to date, in ink that shows no blood-signature, belonging to no name in the Scrolls of Mortality.

**Mission access:** Threat Rating X
**Resources:** *Unknown*
**Camp:** *Unknown*

> *"Time bends around those who walk beyond its measure."*

## How Progression Works

Creeds advance through the Spiral by accumulating **Brotherhood Standing** — earned through field deeds:

| Action | Weight |
|---|---|
| Mission completion (objective fulfilled) | High |
| [[Combat#Relic Recovery\|Relic Recovery]] — relic successfully extracted | Very High |
| [[Combat#Chaos\|Chaos]] — survival to timer end | Moderate |
| Forsaken variant defeated | High |
| [[Combat#4.3 Hourglass Ichor (Special Spawn)\|Hourglass Ichor]] destroyed | Moderate |
| Brother survives mission | Low *(per Brother)* |
| Wipe — all Brothers dead | Negative |
| Early extraction before objective | Slight negative |

Standing accumulates at the **Creed level**, not per-Brother. A surviving Creed carries the weight of its fallen Brothers' deeds forward.

## Camp Progression

As the Creed's rank advances, the **camp** grows physically — from a cold fire in ruins to a Brotherhood outpost to something that feels like a home no one admits they have.

| Rank | Camp Unlock |
|---|---|
| Shrouded Vow (2) | [[Blood Vault]] terminal active |
| Iron Phalanx (3) | Ability upgrade workbench |
| Veilforged (4) | Relic vault and hex-steel forging |
| Eclipsed Sigil (5) | Crypt reading access, map room |
| Nightbound Covenant (6) | Grand Clock monitor, Ichor Core refinery |
| Shadowed Archive (7) | [[Crypt of Knowledge]] annex, magister station |
| Prime Obsidian (9) | Grandmaster sanctum |

## Legacy System *(Planned)*

The long-term goal is **permanent camp buffs inherited from previous Brothers** — legacy that carries forward beyond death.

A Brother who reached Veilforged and fell leaves something behind: not gear, but a trace of their knowledge, their oath, their mark on the Spiral. The exact mechanic is TBD — likely unlocked through the Blood Vault's record of their deeds and the knowledge preserved in their Tomb.

> [!info]
> Full legacy mechanics to be defined. The design intent: the player should feel the accumulated weight of everyone who came before — the Vault's growing list of sealed entries becoming something you draw strength from, not just mourn.

## The Council of Shadows

The Brotherhood is no monolith. The **Council of Shadows** convenes beneath the [[Grand Clock]] at [[Kareth Vall]] once per generation — the most senior Creeds from across the network, gathering to guide the unseen war.

At the Council's centre stands the **Grandmaster** — Keeper of the Crypt, voice of memory, the hand that steadies the hourglass. The Grandmaster does not command; they *remember.* Their authority is not military. It is archival.

What the Council decides shapes which threats are prioritised, which truths are shared downward through the Spiral, and how close to the Grand Clock the Brotherhood's hands are allowed to reach.

---

## Structure — Creeds of the Veil

After [[The Sundering of the Crown]], the reforged Brotherhood fractured deliberately — not from weakness, but by design. Each **Creed** acts autonomously, interpreting the Grand Purpose in its own way:

- Some study forbidden arts to weaponise the Curse
- Others burn such knowledge to prevent its spread
- Some live among the people — healers, monks, librarians, smiths
- Others dwell underground, behind locked sanctums and blood-warded vaults

A Creed's rise is measured not in fame, but in deeds whispered and secrets kept. The Spiral of the Veil tracks collective progression — an unbroken circle of symbols that represents the slow approach toward the inner truth.

> [!quote] Fifth Oath of the Brotherhood
> "We seek no crown, no song, no praise. We serve the world that forgot us."
