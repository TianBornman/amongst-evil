# Lore — Amongst Evil / Grimace Brotherhood

## The World

The world is cursed. When **Akalaer** (the Arch-Warden, shadowed father of necromancy) was struck down by **Alexandr the Iron Tyrant**, the balance of the world fractured. The instant Akalaer's heart stopped, the ground turned cold, the stars dimmed, and the Curse spread across all kingdoms.

The Curse is not divine punishment — it is the consequence of one man's act.

## The Grand Clock

Crafted by **Garin Loctove** (the Grand Forger / god-smith), the Grand Clock is a colossal hourglass of bronze and obsidian gears on the shattered plains of **Kareth Vall**. Twin glass chambers filled with golden sand; the upper bleeds into the lower. Its heartbeat chimes across leagues — each toll marks the shortening of existence.

When Akalaer died, the Clock trembled and its sands began to fall faster. When the last grain falls, time collapses and the world ends.

The Brotherhood's purpose: **slow the fall of the sands**.

## The Grimace Brotherhood

### Origins
- **Alexandr** secretly killed Akalaer to seize forbidden power, triggering the Curse.
- He formed the **Sanctum Vigil** — a holy order to appear as savior while concealing his guilt.
- The Vigil's scholars uncovered the truth in the **Tomb of the First Sin**.
- The Order severed their oaths, burned royal banners, and were branded traitors.
- The King hunted them, erased them from history.
- Survivors met at the ruins of **High Thalenor** and reforged as the **Grimace Brotherhood**.

### Founding Creed (carved in stone beneath their hall)
> "We stand where others flee. We kill what cannot die. We use what should never be touched."

### The Five Oaths (all 15 sworn by every member)
1. "We were born of a lie, but we live by truth."
2. "When gods die, men inherit their curses."
3. "We are the hand that slows the fall of the hourglass."
4. "In blood, we learn. In death, we remember. In darkness, we preserve."
5. "We seek no crown, no song, no praise. We serve the world that forgot us."
6. "Only blood remembers what the tongue must forget."
7. "The living serve. The dead teach. The blood remembers."
8. "Knowledge not born of oath is rot."
9. "The curse feeds on pride — we feed it humility and steel."
10. "We wield the dark not to rule, but to restrain it."
11. "Our hands are stained so others may stay clean."
12. "Every fallen Brother marks a page in eternity."
13. "Truth sleeps in silence; we are its waking blade."
14. "The hour falls for all — we only slow its hand."
15. "For as long as one Brother stands, the world has not yet fallen."

### Purpose
Not victory — **delay and understanding**. They vow to:
- Slow the fall of the Grand Clock's sands
- Unravel the curse's roots and the buried truths
- Discover how to destroy the darkness, even by wielding it

### The Crypt of Knowledge
A blood-bound vault beneath their fortress. Contains the true history of the Curse, the Grand Clock's purpose, and the sins of kings. Blood-ink tomes, ghost-light candles. Access restricted to trusted Brothers.

### Membership & Rituals

**Rite of Names**: Initiates write their name in the Blood Vaults using their own blood. The Vault whispers it — their pulse, magick, and fate are known to the Brotherhood forever.

**Loss of Name**: Those who survive long enough are stripped of their mortal name and renamed by their Creed's Keeper. Examples:
- *The Warden* — protectors of the living
- *The Hollow* — those who sacrifice all humanity to study death
- *Ashblade* — those who burn everything for purity
- *Seraph Null* — those who walk too close to godhood

**Personal Tombs**: Every Brother keeps a tome bound in the skin of the fallen, written in their own blood — journal, confession, and field notes. On death, it is recovered and verified against the Blood Vaults. If confirmed genuine, it is preserved in the Crypt. If not — burned and the ashes cast to the Grand Clock's wind.

### Public Perception
To common folk: a myth. To kings: traitors and heretics. Yet royal messengers secretly seek them when darkness rises. "Obey the hour, lest the Shadows come."

Seven centuries after Akalaer's fall, the nightmares sleep — but only lightly. The Brotherhood knows it is only a pause.

## Key Enemies
